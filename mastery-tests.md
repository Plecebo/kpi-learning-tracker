# Mastery gates — written tests and scoring (weeks 4, 8, 12)

Score every piece with the shared 0–3 rubric (`rubric.md`). Gate passes at average ≥ 2.
A failed gate means redo the artifact, not repeat the month.

## Week 4 — Framework literacy (closed-book)

Write, without notes, in one sitting (~60–90 min):

1. One SMART objective for a real thing, then the 5-criterion check on it (name which SMART
   wording you're using — Doran's original or the modern one — and why).
2. One OKR set: 1 Objective + 3 Key Results for a real change; mark each KR measurable/verifiable.
3. A 4-perspective Balanced Scorecard (≤ 12 measures) for a named org (the estate is fine).

## Week 8 — Derivation & measurement (transfer test: unseen goal)

Given a goal you have NOT practiced on, produce:

1. A full GQM trace (goal with purpose/object/issue/viewpoint → questions → metrics).
2. One operational definition: formula, data source, pull frequency, owner, target, floor/ceiling,
   and how you'd instrument it if the data doesn't exist.
3. A signal-vs-noise verdict on a plotted series with rough control limits: real signal or noise, and why.

## Week 12 — Capstone + retention

1. **Capstone** (the portfolio piece): full stack on one real goal — framework choice + rationale,
   KPIs, operational definitions, baseline, before/after or measurement plan, write-up.
   Target: average ≥ 2 with at least one 3.
2. **Retention quiz:** 10 questions assembled from your weekly "one thing I'd do differently"
   journal lines (they already are your personal weak points). Answer closed-book, then check.
3. Record both scores here, then write the consolidation note (week 12 artifact) and hand it to
   the librarian for the wiki.
