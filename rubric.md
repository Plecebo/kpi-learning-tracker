# Self-assessment rubric — 0–3, same scale all 12 weeks

Score each week's check-in artifact against these behavioral anchors. Score honestly: calibration
is part of the skill being learned. One sentence of justification is required with every score.

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| **0 — absent** | Artifact not produced. Record it as 0, not blank. |
| **1 — present** | Artifact exists but is generic; it could have been written without doing the week's reading. |
| **2 — applied** | Uses the week's framework correctly on real estate (or other real) data; concrete numbers, names, sources. |
| **3 — critiqued** | Applied AND self-critiqued: names a weakness in the artifact, a way the metric could be gamed, or a counter/guardrail metric. |

Gate thresholds (weeks 4, 8, 12): pass at average ≥ 2 across the gate's pieces, with at least one 3
required at the week 12 capstone.
